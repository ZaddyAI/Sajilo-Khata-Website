# Sajilo Khata Light Green Modern Design System

The Sajilo Khata design system uses a fresh, light, airy emerald & mint aesthetic. It avoids heavy, dark green slabs (such as dark forest footers or murky backgrounds) in favor of crisp white surfaces, soft mint tints, and vibrant light emerald action highlights.

## 1. Color Palette

### Primary Brand Colors
- **Vibrant Light Emerald**: `#10B981` (Primary brand buttons, badges, navigation highlights)
- **Forest Emerald Accent**: `#059669` (Hover states, high-contrast headings, icon emphasis)
- **Mint Accent**: `#34D399` (Secondary gradients and pill highlights)
- **Soft Mint Surface**: `#ECFDF5` (Pills, badges, icon backgrounds)
- **Airy Canvas**: `#F7FCF9` (Main website background)
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Card surfaces, navbar pill, phone frames)
- **Subtle Mint Border**: `#E6F4ED` / `#D1FAE5` (Card borders, dividers)

### Typography
- **Headings & Ink**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900 - Crisp, clear, high contrast)
- **Body & Secondary Text**: `#475569` (Slate 600 - Readable, modern)
- **Muted Text**: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400 - Labels, footnotes)

### Finance Semantics
- **Income & Growth**: `#10B981`
- **Expenses & Outflow**: `#F43F5E`
- **Warning & Pending**: `#F59E0B`

## 2. Gradients
- **Primary Light Emerald Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981 0%, #059669 100%)`
- **Gradient Text Highlight**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #059669 0%, #10B981 50%, #34D399 100%)`
- **Luminous Mint Surface Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #F0FDF4 50%, #ECFDF5 100%)`

## 3. Shadows & Elevation
- **Card Shadow**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.05)`
- **Card Hover Shadow**: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`
- **Button Shadow**: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`
